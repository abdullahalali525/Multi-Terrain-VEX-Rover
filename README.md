# Multi-Terrain-VEX-Rover
One of my PLTW Engineering Projects.
This is my Multi_terrain Rover part 1!! In this part I'll explain the concept and details of this project and what it does.

Main Cause and Background: 
-one of the main projects in my robotics unit, my partners consisting of 3 members were tasked to build a robot strictly using gears and sprockets to finish. The robot had to move a distance of 50m without stalling. 
Process and Building:
-Upon building we sketched a different type completely, we excluded the possibility of creating a 4-wheeler type. 

What it is: The continuous high-traction rubberized tank tread wrapped around the sprockets with gears in the middle who are spun by a motor.
Purpose: Distributes the rover's weight over a larger surface area to maximize ground contact, improve stability, and prevent slipping across uneven or loose terrain.
Overall:
Architecture: VEX Robotics Component System

Key Features:

Drivetrain: Triangular continuous track (tank tread) assembly optimized for high ground clearance and multi-terrain traction.

Structure: Modular C-channel aluminum frame designed for torsional rigidity and low center of mass.

Control System: Microcontroller-driven closed-loop actuation utilizing integrated motor encoders and digital sensor inputs.

The rover was able to drive over small obstacles like small bumps and functioned continuously the whole entire period. The project took about 5 classes or 3-4 hours of building and actual testing.
